To My Teddy ❤❤

For my sweet, beautiful, chubby princess

Hey Teddy, surprise. This letter is just to remind you how much I appreciate you, how much I love you, and how deeply grateful I am to have you in my life.

I am almost in tears while writing this because I keep thinking about the times I have not been the boyfriend you deserved. Sometimes I sit and wonder if you really deserved someone as difficult as me, but that is not mine alone to decide. What I do know is this: I truly love you, and sometimes I wonder if telling you with words will ever be enough.

I know keeping up with me can be tiring. I know I can be stubborn, emotional, annoying, and hard to understand. But we are still here, four months down, and I am still blessed to call you mine. You are my caring, kind, loving, motherly, and warm girl. You are beautiful inside and out, and you make love feel soft even when life feels heavy.

God knows how long we will be together. That thought can be scary, I know. But for as long as I am yours and you are mine, I promise to make this love memorable and unforgettable for both of us. I promise to keep learning you, choosing you, and trying harder for you.

Sometimes our relationship gets scary, but maybe that is part of its beauty. We get to know each other more. We get to grow through the hard days and laugh through the silly ones. I love every version of you: my friend, my mentor, my therapist, my comfort, my favorite rage-baiter, and most of all, my girl.

I wish I could give you a tight hug right now because you deserve one. You deserve softness, patience, honesty, loyalty, and a love that makes you feel safe. Baby, just know that I love you much more than I say, much more often than you realize, and much deeper than words can carry.

Thank you for staying, for loving me, for being you, and for making my heart feel at home.

I love you, Teddy. Always. ❤❤
